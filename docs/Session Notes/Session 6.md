### 2. Korvos’s Armor Acquisition

The armorsmith delivers a heavy, multi-form magical robe to Korvos instead of standard plate armor.

* **Base Stats:** AC 19, Fire Resistance, Immune to being disarmed.
* **Shape-Shifting:** Requires concentration to shift appearances (e.g., Plate Mail, Robe, etc.).
* **Active Ability:** Cast *Fly* once per day.
* **Defensive Feature (1/day):** When reduced to 1 HP, a "Death Warrant" triggers HP drops to 1 instead of 0 (the *next* attack can be fatal). Upon triggering, all creatures in a set radius must succeed on a **DC 17 Saving Throw** or become Frightened.
* **Lore:** The expectations of three separate deities now rest upon Korvos.
