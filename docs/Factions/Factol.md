A **factol** is the title given to the leader of a faction, although it does occasionally apply to sect leaders as well. Not all factions have a factol, depending on their internal structure.

The current factols of the factions of Sigil are:

- The Athar: Factol Terrance 
- The Bleak Cabal: Factol Lhar 
- The Doomguard: Factol Pentar 
- The Fated: Factol Rowan Darkwood 
- The Fraternity of Order: Factol Hashkar 
- The Harmonium: Factol Sarin 
- The Heralds of Dust: Factol Skall 
- The Mercykillers: Factol Alisohn Nilesia 
- The Mind's Eye: Factol Saladryn
- The Ring Givers: Jeremo the Natterer
- The Society of Sensation: Factol Erin Darkflame Montgomery 
- The Transcendent Order: Factol Rhys 

The Free League and Hands of Havoc do not have factols. The Incanterium may have one, but their identity is unknown.

O'pok the Fawning runs a history and propaganda museum in the Clerk's Ward dedicated to factols past and present.
