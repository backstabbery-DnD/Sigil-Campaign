    Session 3

    Just leaving the courthouse - we are going to the bar to meet up with Guvner Neverbloom. We received a Mimir for promising to talk to the shapeshifting hooker on behalf of the guvners. We are also doing a favor for the guvners where we find out about the mercykillers accepting bribes from slavers - this gets farmer pait and petra home, and then an additional favor for the The Astral Lady / Guvner Neverbloom.

    We meet a Balor entering the courtroom - i say "good luck in there" in abyssal. Balor, says i should know where i'm going to spend my afterlife - i ask him if he has any suggestions, he asks me if i heard of the blood wars - i say no and ask him to explain further. I tell him about my faith and he is shocked, i should read up on my faith more. He says his place is ineviitable, i agree and say I'm inevitable. We part ways saying - stay inevitable.

    Liberty Hall Tavern - Farmer Pait & Petra are having a conversation on the way there. Talking about his dog. We hear a ghastly shriek from a tower a little ways away. The tower is like a big flute, the wind is making the shriek. 3 Harmonium are outside the tavern playing cards. The tower shrieking is a clock, it shrieks every hour on the hour. One of the harmonium guys who is named Koin offers to buy us our first round.

    The inside of the tavern is conventional ( this is essentially a cop bar ) - we line up at the bar. There is a gnome who is almost dead laying on one of the tables. The barkeep and harmonium have called the dustmen to come collect the body, but the gnome is not dead yet. It is explained that you can sign your dead body over to the dustmen for 500 gold, they put a tattoo on your neck and then when you die they get your body. The bar only serves milk / water / mild stuff - so we order flights of milk. Korvos casts detect good and evil - we finally find out Rhys is lawful evil.

    Rhys is reading the book he found in the crypt // rolls charisma saving throw // nat 20 - more information about a dark primordial magic. Translating the words from whatever dark weirdness back to common he feels power with each word. The books knowledge is tied to demonology and it was owned by a demon. It has a list of true names ( could we find the true name of the The Astral Lady? ), he feels a familiarity with summoning a Vrock.

    What does the special spellscar jerky look like with detect good & evil - its pure purple, not good or evil.

    We get our flights of milk, and we drink some with seemingly no ill effects. Except for Lonkee who double fists all the milk and is going to end up having the shits.

    3 dustmen walk in, a halfling, a mummy and a stitched together goblin. The halfling is lawful evil, the undead are chaotic evil.

    Korvos heals the gnome for 15 hit points and they gasp awake. Dustmen are pretty much the morticians of Sigil. The halflings name is Paternost and is seemingly commanding the undead that are following her.

    I ask the gnome that Korvos revivied what her name is - Glib Lostwin. We get sidetracked with Lonkee potentially being a shit cannon weapon and feedling her more cheese.

    After a couple hours and a short rest Guvner Neverbloom shows up. She gives us the lazy, good for nothing ( her words ) Mimir. Mimirasks Guvner Neverbloom if he helps us will he be free, she tells the mimir to ask the snake. The The Astral Lady has the title of The Astral Lady ( we were previously referring to her as mysterious mage ). Her main residence is out of time. The mimir seems to know some stuff about the Astral Lady. Korvos wants to kill some dustmen.

    We tell Guvner Neverbloom we want a warrant - no. We want a letter of authority - no. We want Mercykillers uniforms - no. So then i convince her to let us raid their evidence vault and i roll a 20 so she agrees.

    Lonkee finally shits herself.

    Guvner Neverbloom brings us back mercykillers armor from the evidence vault. Rhys converts a magic item into a bag of holding and we stuff the disguises in there. Our plan is to find "Marcella" of the fated and pose as corrupt mercykillers looking to help her cause and once inside hopefully find the other corrupt mercykillers.

    Korvos tells the Harmonium guy Koin that we are doing a secret investigation into the mercykillers. Guvner Neverbloom has emptied a jar of equipment onto the table in front of us. This is what prompted Korvos to try and get the harmonium on our side.

    I give farmer pait 30 gold and a specific business card so i can track him later from a distance.

    Guvner Neverbloom - we are to never mention the guvners if we get caught. Suggests we go to the seedy side of Sigil and possibly the prison to investigate the Mercykillers.

    The Smoldering Corpse bar is where the mercykillers hang out. On our way there we look for an Inn.

    We pass through a Chessentan ( greek mythology inspired ) area of sigil. We see a temple to Zeus, with people exercising in the courtyard. We overhear what they are preparing for, they are trying to pull a statue down.

    Lonkee is going to wrestle one of the greek exercise guys. Lonkee absolutely dominates him, suplexs him right out of the ring. They start chanting HERO. So they give Lonkee and her heroic companions first crack at the statue, I guess we have to topple it?

    Its 2 Stone Golems. We attack and they have a lot of resistances. We get one prone, but that doesn't seem good enough, we have to defeat it. As we keep attacking we start making performance checks and flourish our attacks which the crowd and the master of ceremonies seem to love.

    We finally bloody #2 Stone Golem it takes a knee, next round #1 Stone Golem takes a knee. The master of ceremony and the crowd go crazy. The crowd cheers, they try to douse us in olive oil. Most of us move out of the way, except Lonkee. We have Zeus's favor.

    Rewards coming in next session.
