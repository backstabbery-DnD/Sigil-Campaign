    Session Highlights

    The Temple Visit

    Korvos and Lonkee headed to a temple dedicated to Kelemvor. Korvos tithed a tenth of his gold (177 gp) to an automaton servant at the door to gain entry, offering thanks to Kelemvor for aiding his holy mission. Inside, a minotaur follower was completely starstruck to meet Lonkee, who promptly celebrated by sharing mayonnaise from her Alchemy Jug with everyone.

    The Mercykiller Heist

    Meanwhile, Rhy and I set out to acquire some gear from the Mercykillers. We found a garrison and Rhys created a distraction, allowing me to slip inside unnoticed disguised as a mercykiller. I thoroughly intimidated a young recruit into handing over six Dimensional Shackles and a Catchpole—frightening him so badly he ruined his pants. I ordered him to change, grabbed the gear, and snuck out.

    Dimensional Shackles : we now have 6 of these, they are considered contraband for anyone other than mercykillers or harmonium to have. 18kgp a piece.
    Catchpole : The Catchpole exerts a Silence effect on target creatures when used. Korvos : he is waiting for armor that will take 1 week?

    The Wager at New Tyr

    Regrouping, we set out to find Yu, a shapeshifting worker, heading toward her last known location in New Tyr. On the way, we stumbled upon a crowd gathered around a horrific scene: a weretiger was actively eviscerating a woman while a circle of onlookers watched. Smell of blood filled the air.

    Korvos raised his Lightbringer to illuminate the scene and cast Calm Emotions. I moved in to heal the victim, but she rejected the aid and healed herself. Introducing herself as Loretta Roots, she explained that she sells herself to be mauled by dangerous beasts as part of a betting ring—meaning she effectively cannot die, and our intervention ruined the wager.

    Loretta challenged us to try killing her ourselves. Before accepting, we asked if she knew Yu's whereabouts; she pointed us south toward the Colosseum. Lonkee and I each placed a 50 gp bet and attacked. I pinned her with the Catchpole while Lonkee swung her Chaos Axe, triggering wild magic by rolling a 1. Lonkee dealt massive damage, but Loretta ultimately outlasted us, and we lost the bet.

    Locating Yu & The Colosseum Champion

    We proceeded to the Colosseum to resume our search for Yu. To reach her, we had to confront the Colosseum Champion, a wood elf who happens to be her boyfriend.

    We escorted Lonkee into the arena with grand fanfare. Along the way, Lonkee's hat began speaking to her. Since she responded out loud to the hat's inner voice, the rest of us were convinced she was losing her mind.

    I spoke to the Champion in Elvish, assuring him we only wanted to talk with Yu, and he agreed to let us speak. Yu explained her history with Marcella, arguing that she ( Marcella ) was just doing the best she could with a bad hand, noting that the Dustmen never purchase her services anyway. With the Champion's help, we convinced Yu to shapeshift into Marcella's appearance, giving us a visual profile of our target.

    • Exhibition Match: We agreed to return for an exhibition fight against the Champion on a future night after Korvos receives his +1 Plate Mail, which will allow us to place bets on the match.
    • Tracking Marcella: I asked Yu if Marcella wore any distinct jewelry. Yu confirmed she wears a holy symbol of Loviatar (the Maiden of Pain, consort to Bane). Yu shifted to replicate the pin, giving me a clear visual to cast Locate Object.

    The House of the Gryphon & Unsavory Encounters

    We asked for a local tavern recommendation to set up a home base while waiting for Marcella and the Champion's fight invitation. We were directed to the House of the Gryphon tavern, located near the Hive.

    Upon arrival, I cast Locate Object focused on the Loviatar pin, picking up roughly 90 hits within a 1,000-foot radius.

    While securing rooms, the bartender became enamored with Lonkee and tried to recruit us into the Revolutionary League. Meanwhile, I got into a heated dispute with a Tabaxi patron. Rhy stepped in and bought the Tabaxi off to defuse the situation, while Korvos held me back, urging me to focus on the mission. As Korvos said "focus," I subtly cast Druidcraft on the Tabaxi to make him smell strongly of cat urine.

    Despite the mess, Lonkee ended up sleeping with the Tabaxi... who somehow ended up pregnant afterward. For absolute fuck's sake.
