    Session 4

    Session Overview & Recap

    • New Additions: Fiora ( Zoe ) a Paladin & Flatfoot ( Sam ) a Rogue joined the party.
    • Reference: See the Group Loot Discord channel for stats on the Golden Laurel of Zeus.

    1. The Feast of Zeus

    Gorick Fablehorn, a satyr, hosts a grand celebration while a Cleric performs a magical ritual on a large statue of Zeus. Playful nymphs emerge from the water with bread and wine, magically materializing a dining table right above the surface before serving a succulent boar roast.

    One nymph stands out—she appears more human, carries a shortbow with a partially emptied quiver, and looks mildly injured. Visibly disgruntled, she argues quietly but pointedly with the Cleric. When he dismisses her, she spits at the foot of Zeus's statue, causing the air to crackle with electric tension at his divine displeasure.

    I hand out wine and fake drinking while the rest of the party feasts. The magical meal cures everyone's status ailments, grants immunity to the frightened condition, and leaves them invigorated. While everyone is distracted, I get Rhys drunk and steal the demonic book from him; Korvos then successfully casts Remove Curse on it.

    2. The Changeling & The Interrupted Feast

    Four dancers take position at the corners of the table—three humans and an air genasi. One female dancer feels strangely familiar. Following History and Religion checks, we realize she is styled to look like Aphrodite.

    In hindsight, this was the Changeling named Yu. Though we failed our Insight checks in the moment and she ran off before we could act, finding her later might give us a lead on what Marcella looks like.

    3. Aereston's Challenge

    A commotion breaks out in the courtyard where we previously fought the statues. Aereston—a proud, towering Chessentian larger than anyone we’ve met—enters leading a group of 12 followers. He loudly demands to see Zeus's champion and refuses to believe it could be Lonkee.

    In response, Lonkee steps up, scrambles up Aereston's back, and snatches the Laurel Wreath of Zeus directly off his head. Aereston—who speaks exclusively in the third person—challenges us to a fight for the laurel, and we accept.

    4. Battle & Divine Transfer

    Five of us engage in combat while Flatfoot the Rogue flees to hide in a corner. When Aereston begins to concede and ramble, I fire one last shot at him. He abruptly explodes into golden lightning, damaging everyone within 10 feet and knocking Korvos unconscious.

    The Laurel officially transfers its magic to Lonkee. Feeling guilty, Lonkee tries to apologize and offers Aereston a +1 sickle. Aereston refuses the weapon, announcing he is abandoning adventuring to do some soul-searching, and walks away. Lonkee fully dedicates herself as Zeus's new champion.

    5. The Demonic Book

    Korvos ( recently healed ) and I show the demonic book to the Cleric of Zeus to ask how to destroy it. He places it in a brazier and uses magical fire to burn it, but the ritual unexpectedly transforms it into a Zeus-aligned Necronomicon instead.

    We slip the altered book back into Rhys's pocket, and I write "Demon Book" on the front cover for good measure.

    6. Interacting with Fiora (The Paladin)

    We speak with Fiora, a Shadar-kai Paladin (Oath of the Watchers). She has pale skin, pitch-black eyes, tattoos, piercings, and wears black chainmail with silver highlights and black feathers. Her hooded mask resembles a griffon.

    She takes a drink from Korvos's flask but succeeds on her saving throw. She reveals she was sent by her temple to find two ancient twin souls so the Raven God can guide them to the afterlife.

    7. Gorick’s Warning & The Laurel’s Burden

    Gorick cautions Lonkee not to grow arrogant now that she has Zeus's favor and hears his voice. He explains that the true test wasn't the statues or the athletic trials—the real trial is enduring the weight and responsibility of Zeus's gift. Lonkee now has the Golden Laurel for exactly one year.
