    Session 8

    Below is a quick summary of last night's shenanigans...

    @NSLWඞ If you can let me know correct name spellings or correct anything I misstate, that'd be appreciated!

    We met our housemates, whom it turns out have been hear for a year or so, and include: - Sir Roland Olisen (age 45), a Human Banneret (Fighter) who gives us an amusing backstory of being the 11th son in a house of magic, and trying everything they could to get magic (tried to be every magic using class and was denied/refused every. single. time.) - Basis Varmoon, an Autumn Eladrin Elf Swarmkeeper (Ranger), who has insects crawling all over/in/through her, and can talk with them (was surprised Kaisarion couldn't talk to them) - Brass Harp, a Rock Gnome College of Creation (Bard), who was raised by Storm Giants, who was often overlooked/not heard by his siblings, and as such when he found human sized instruments, learned to play them to make enough noise to be noticed - Mist Connections, a Glasya Tiefling, who gives us absolutely nothing (we've assumed she's a Rogue of some sort) - Their dead Cleric in the urn (we forgot to ask further about them, so no name/further details yet)

    Kaisarion asks if they were able to find us a Black Marketeer to take on our pilfered wares, and Mist Connections merely says yes, and they'll find you when ready

    There's a massive celebration going on outside, and when we ask them about it, they mention it's the Celebration of Nothing, informing us that there was previously a celebration of something, and the Lady of Pain took offense to the 'worship' and slaughtered tons of people. So now there's a yearly festival to celebrate 'nothing at all', and people celebrate however they want, do whatever they want, and no one is celebrating anything, so The Lady of Pain ignores it

    The housemates wander off to the festival in one direction, we go another, and stumble across an Ent Sensate, who offers us any experience we want to experience (we're not beta testers this time, we're paying customers, so it's totally super safe and no danger at all (/they'll pay our medical costs and reimburse us in the unlikely event something goes horribly wrong))

    Rhys asks for another experience of death, hoping to gain more insight into his near death experience from before, so we end up in something call The Slaughter of the Kittens

    Rhys, Lonkee, and Kaisarion put on the headsets and go in, Fiora says hell no to that after watching what happened last time with Rhys, and we assumed Korvos similarly says hell no to it, and instead watches over the group/wanders (feel free to correct us if you'd have chosen otherwise); Fiora watches through a small orb what happens/we do

    In the simulation, the 3x are shown to be in the jungles of Chult, with some NPC commander telling us to kill everyone in the nearby Tabaxi village, the village including a proud Tabaxi Paladin, and two dozen Tabaxi children crying and afraid

    *We're told that the original battle was many times larger on both sides, but this is scaled down to the size of our party for 'our enjoyment' We roll initiative and start the fight, the Paladin comes to us and Kaisarion and Rhys start fighting it, but Lonkee rushes to the children and goes to start slapping them. She goes to close her eyes to crit fail and get her axe to do a thing (it was decided to rewrite how the axe works going forward, and rolling a 1 will be a critical success, and rolling a 20 will be wildmagic happening, but that's after this session), and upon seeing this, the Ent offers Free Play mode, allowing us to Crit Succeed/Fail at will (disabling high score mode), so Lonkee crit fails, and due to wild magic (The Deck of Daddy Things), immediately changes from Chaotic Neutral, to Lawful Evil, then due to another Wild Magic surge, becomes obsessed with 'ball sports', so she changes her stance to one shot the head off a child, then kicks it like a soccer ball into another child, killing it

    Kaisarion sees a small familiar looking Druid type child Tabaxi and murders it horribly, then starts to question his life choices of wanting to murder all Tabaxi, and upon realizing the group is in Free Play mode, uses the excuse of 'this is no longer a challenge' to dip out of the fight

    Rhys during this time is seeing the Paladin crying for the dead children decides this is beyond messed up and tries to crit kill the Paladin, and upon just not doing enough damage, decides to Nope out of this as it's just too messed up even if it isn't real Upon leaving the simulation first, Rhys finds Fiora trying to proselytizing to the Nib Nab #6, with mixed results, as the Nib Nab keeps getting distracted Lonkee coming back to reality goes back to Chaotic Neutral, and suddenly does not feel ok with what she did in the simulation, so when Korvos offers her his flask, she downs that, then wanders off to find more booze

    The Ent seems confused and want to apologize that we didn't seem to enjoy our experience, but we all just kinda wander off (huh, turns out we are starting to think the Sensates are kinda messed up people and maybe we shouldn't be dealing with them?)

    As we wander off, an arrow whizzes past Kaisarion, who dodges it, and hits a nearby wall, with a note dropping to the ground, and there being no trace of any arrow. The note includes a drawing showing a location, and assuming this is from the Black Marketeer, we head in that direction to see what we find

    We come across an alleyway with a sleeping Marilith, and Kaisarion quickly casts Pass Without a Trace, and we sneak past without issue, finding a door; Kaisarion(Rhys?) notices some handholds leading up the building instead, and we decide to climb that instead of go through the front door, and barely a few rungs up, we each disapparate, and reappear inside a building (the one we were outside of?)

    We choose a door, noticing and avoiding the trap, then find ourselves within a room with 'not a Tabaxi' (we eventually find it's a Rakshasa), who seems to be our Black Marketeer. They are calm and confident, not seeming too perturbed by us coming in the 'employee entrance'. We figured Korvos would cast a 'Zone of Truth', so everyone could be confident we're as trustworthy on both sides as possible, and after we do the Rakshasa tells us they're a 60' tall Myconid (@NSLWඞ and what else?), quickly proving the Zone of Truth means nothing to them (turns out Rakshasa can't be affected by 6th level and below spells if they don't want to be), so we quickly give up on that. We go back and forth about wanting to sell the dimensional shackles, having 6x for sale, and negotiating their cut, we eventually settle on 50%, and they state we can either sell them for 18k each now, of perhaps get a better deal if we're not in a rush, when we agree to that, they mention it'll likely take a month or so. We also ask if he'd have interest in 'a new unique species!' in selling the Nib Nabs, and he says he's not much for the flesh trade, and doesn't have any use for them

    After that, we mention we're looking for Marchella, and while initially the Rakshasa says they're willing to help, but would prefer we keep Marchella alive if possible, we let slip we're mostly looking for the Mercy Killers she's bribing, and he offers to just give us their names instead. He mentions that Marchella is just a small fish in a larger web of people, and that killing her would annoy some people he/we probably would rather not bother with, so in exchange for those names, he requests a future favor. After deliberations, we begrudgingly agree, but he wants a handshake, and while Rhys vehemently refuses that, Fiora agrees, shaking on it (we didn't quite know at the time that Rakshasa are Fiends, but it still seemed questionable that he was requiring a handshake on it all)

    With that concluded, the Raksha mentions to Fiora that he has something for her, says it's been here since before his time (when questioned how long he's been here, 'no idea, centuries tend to blur together...'), and Fiora is just as confused as the rest of us who knew she'd be here, when she's only been here for a few weeks/months at most. The Rakshasa wanders off, then returns with a large chest, covered in years/decade/centuries/more of dust on it

    Upon opening, Fiora finds a ton of things, including:

    • The warhammer Epilogue (+2 warhammer, all cold damage, adds 1d6 extra cold on normal attacks, adds another 1d10 cold damage on critical hits)
    • A wand of Secrets
    • A Potion of Greater Invisibility
    • 3x Potions of Greater Healing
    • A Driftglobe
    • A Potion of Fire Resistance
    • A Scrolls of Protection from Elementals
    • Boots of Elvenkind
    • 6,000 gold
    • A small note

    Before leaving Lonkee asks if the Rakshasa could get us more jerky, and while he cannot directly source it, he does mention he can provide us with the location of a group of survivors of the Spellpague, and we can investigate ourselves. Lonkee agrees and shakes on it Rhys mentions creating a magical bottle to capture a magical creature, and the Rakshasa mentioned that sounds like it would require an antimagic field of sorts, so we should talk with someone who knows how to cast Antimagic Field (8th level spell for Cleric/Wizard). We thank him and decide we should visit the priest of Zeus at some point to discuss

    We all then head back to the house, each making our own way

    • Rhys plies his trade as a surgeon/healer for those enjoying the festival, healing the drunk, the ones who have appendages almost cut off, and one who shows up looking healthy, and he immediately offers him cocaine to continue the party. He receives donations as payment, include, 51g, and a Scroll of Slow (meant to allow someone to bang for twice their normal time, positively affecting their satisfaction rolls, @Fiora (Zoe) see the document mentioned last night, in the 'dm-announcements' channel )
    • Lonkee heads home signing autographs as she goes, making 5g per signature (random squiggles and 'X's of course), making 200g
    • Kaisarion heads home rethinking his life choices based on The Slaughter of the Kittens, and on the way there comes across a dog faced monstrosity (likely one of Lonkee's offspring with the dog race person), who is dragging a human body with it; upon a low Survival roll Kaisarion isn't able to charm it this time, but it wanders off with the body without fighting
    • Fiora visits the Sensate's local building/HQ, which has physical memories flowing through the building/columns/pipes, and asks an on duty Kenku (named 'Goodnight') about finding any information on Ancient Twin Souls; without having more to go on the Kenku is unable to assist, so Fiora decides to come back when she as more details
    • Korvos is assumed to have gone back to the Colosseum to further find why putting the spirits there to rest did not work as expected
